BG | Separating Incoming and Post-process Evidence

Which measurements are available before grinding, and which explain quality only afterwards?

0.5638Incoming classifier ROC-AUC
0.9988Post-process diagnostic ROC-AUC
4,000Same evaluation rows

My contribution

Proposed separating incoming from post-grinding measurements, shared comparisons with teammates, and organized observations, possible causes and follow-up checks.

Actions and deliverables

Separated the original team discussion from the later AI-assisted validation record below. Incoming measurements and post-process diagnostic measurements are evaluated separately; a high diagnostic score is not presented as incoming prediction.

What the evidence supports

On the same 4,000-row evaluation set, the selected incoming classifier had ROC-AUC 0.5638. A post-process diagnostic model reached 0.9988 but uses measurements unavailable before grinding. Pressure, RPM, chuck condition and SSD remain follow-up measurement topics, not confirmed causes.

Educational synthetic data, 20,000 rows. The historical RF importance near 90% is not a causal defect contribution. TTV measures thickness variation; it is not a direct bow/warp measurement. No production recipe or yield improvement is claimed.

AI contribution: implementation, calculations and figures were AI-assisted; personal actions are listed above.

05. OOD와 저장된 평가 사례의 검토량

범위 밖·joint support 희박·결측은 REVIEW로 확률/구간을 제공하지 않습니다. 새 Lot ID만으로 OOD 처리하지 않으며, 지원 범위 안이라도 합성-domain 연구 전용입니다. PSI ≥0.2는 가정 경보이며 실제 시간 drift 검증을 대체하지 않습니다.

공개 페이지의 도구는 저장된 평가 예측에 임계값을 적용하는 예시입니다. 새 입력의 모델 추론이나 개입 실험이 아닙니다.

06. 검증 경로와 한계
BG incoming → 그룹 분리 → 기준모델 비교 → OOD review. 추가 계측은 가설별 unit·timestamp·tool·wafer 위치를 함께 기록하고 독립적 실험으로 검증해야 합니다.
flowchart LR
A[Incoming measurements] --> B[BG risk model]
B --> C{Support check}
C --> D[Research risk / interval]
C --> E[Human review]
F[After Crack / Scratch] --> G[Post diagnostic only]
95% 잔차 반폭: \(q_{0.95}=\operatorname{Quantile}_{\lceil(n+1)0.95\rceil}(|y-\hat y|)\). 이 식은 동일 합성 분포의 교환가능성을 가정합니다.
